ACME Office Supplies — Refund and Returns Policy

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery for unused items in their original packaging. Items customized with a customer's logo are not eligible for return unless they arrived damaged or defective.

To request a return, the customer must provide the order number and a short explanation. Approved refunds are normally processed within 5–7 business days after the returned item is inspected. Shipping fees are not refundable unless the return is due to an error by ACME Office Supplies.

Damaged or defective products should be reported within 7 calendar days after delivery, with photographs where possible. This policy is a fictional example for software testing only.
